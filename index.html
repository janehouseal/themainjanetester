Jane Houseal

Introducing Jane

Jane is a movement journalist and fact-checker based in Brooklyn, NY.

Jane is currently pursuing a Masters in Journalism at CUNY Craig Newmark Graduate School of Journalism. Most recently, she worked as an Editorial Intern and Fact-Checker at In These Times and served as the Wisconsin 2024 Election Correspondent for Teen Vogue, where she covered politics from a youth perspective, focusing on social movements and the people behind them.
